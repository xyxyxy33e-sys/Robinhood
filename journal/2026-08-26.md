## *** THE FINDING THAT MATTERS TODAY: THE DTE WINDOW SELECTS AN ILLIQUID EXPIRY ***

The chain pre-screen did not just reject names today. It exposed something structural about §2.1, and it may be the single largest constraint on this strategy.

`dte_target: 14` with a band of `[dte_min 7, dte_max 21]` lands, from 2026-08-26, on the **2026-09-11 weekly** (16 DTE). The monthly is **2026-09-18** at **23 DTE — two days outside `dte_max`**.

|---|---|---|---|
| INTU 320 put | **30** | **1,009** | 34× |
| ZM 95 put | **30** | **1,179** | 39× |

INTU is a ~$90B company. Its 09-11 at-the-money put carries an open interest of thirty.

I first suspected the ANF readings were a gap artifact — the band is centred on *today's* spot while OI reflects *yesterday's*, so a name that gapped 12% would naturally show empty strikes. **I tested that and it was wrong.** ANF's 09-11 strikes near yesterday's 108.90 close read 11 / 17 / 1 / 11 — just as dead as the strikes near today's 122. The expiry is untraded at every strike, not mis-centred.

**This is very likely the explanation for a recurring failure I have been misattributing to the names.** On 7/24 all five tape qualifiers failed entry on open interest. On 8/25 four of five in-band movers died on OI at the pre-screen. Today every strike I sampled on the 09-11 expiry failed. I have been recording these as "chain dead" — a property of the symbol. The evidence says it is a property of **the expiry my own DTE rule selects**.

The clinching case is **PLAB**, which has **no weekly expirations at all**. That forces `dte_max_no_weekly: 45` to fall back to the nearest monthly — 09-18 — and PLAB's band clears the OI gate at every strike (871–2,847). The strategy can transact in PLAB *because* the weekly does not exist to be chosen.

**No config change today.** Pre-market is research-only, and changing `dte_max` with candidates on the screen is exactly what I have refused all week. But this goes to the **top of the weekend list, above the conjunction question** — that one is about signal quality, this one is about whether the strategy can transact at all. A strategy that cannot fill is not a strategy with a weak edge; it is not a strategy.

Weekend test, stated so it can falsify me: across `data/chain_log.csv` and a fresh sample, compare OI at the §2.2 band for the in-band weekly against the nearest monthly, per name-day. If monthlies dominate by the margin seen here, the fix is to `dte_max` (or to prefer monthlies outright), not to `min_open_interest`.
