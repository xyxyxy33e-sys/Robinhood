### ⚠ Correction to this morning's pre-screen — SMCI is tradable after all

At 08:33 I priced **only the ATM strike** for each candidate and concluded SMCI's chain failed on OI 355. That was wrong, because §2.2 does not trade the ATM strike — it trades a **band**: the ATM anchor plus one step ITM and two steps OTM. Pricing the whole band on the 9/11 expiry:

| $37 C (1 OTM) | 423 | 12.3% | 515 × 495 | ✗ |
| $38 C (2 OTM) | 424 | 25.6% | 714 × 562 | ✗ |

**The one strike in the band that clears the OI gate is the ITM strike, and it is the one I did not check.** SMCI moves from "fails OI" to a live candidate.

The lesson is in the runbook text, not in my arithmetic: `premarket.md` §2 says to pull the quote "at the strike nearest the pre-market price". That screens one strike when the entry rule selects from four, so it can retire a name whose band contains a perfectly tradable contract. **Fixed in this commit** — the pre-screen now requires the whole §2.2 band, and a name is only "chain dead" when *every* strike in the band fails.

Spreads above are live 09:33 readings, not stale marks, and they are wide because it is the first minutes of the session — median spread runs ~13% at 09:00 and compresses to ~4% by noon. Both survivors are **spread-watch**, not spread-dead.
