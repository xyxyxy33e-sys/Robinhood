## 6. Findings for the weekend review

1. **`strike_search_steps_itm` = 1 is too narrow on a fast-moving underlying.** Demonstrated three times today on LRCX alone (call side OI at $340 not $330; put side at $320 not $315/$310). Consider widening the ITM reach or anchoring the band on the OI distribution rather than on spot.
2. **§3.2 should state explicitly that §3.4 binds under `dry_run`.** A literal reading sends a paper cycle from the fill straight to §4, which would have booked an entry the live system was required to refuse. I applied the abort to the paper book deliberately; the runbook should not rely on that judgement.
3. **Spreads oscillate; they do not trend.** SMCI's $37P printed 12.77% -> 5.13% -> 14.05% -> 7.66% -> 16.51% in one morning. A stale spread carries no sign, not merely no precision.
4. **Two undocumented §1.3 warm-up gaps** — the 3-consecutive-closes test needs four bars, and the volume baseline needs bar index 9 (~10:15 ET). Neither is written down.
5. **Flat-close handling is undefined in two places** — the close sequence and structure test (a). OXY hit it on (a) today; XOM hit it on the sequence on 8/18. Define it once, for both.
6. **Bar-pull helper** — eighth instance in three sessions of a truncated session start or wrong interval, every one caught by attention rather than design. Still the top item.
7. **Sizing is silent on very expensive contracts.** Had LRCX's chain passed, the ATM $310P at ~$1,558 would have allowed exactly one contract — a different risk profile from the 8-18 lots this book has been sizing.
8. **New: two agents, one account, opposite views.** The other strategy bought LRCX and XOM long today while this one was working puts on LRCX, and bought three names this one excludes on earnings.
